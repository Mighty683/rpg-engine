# Game master (MG) — provisional persona

**Status:** Simulated, not based on participant interviews. Created 2026-11-04; updated 2026-11-09.

## Persona

The game master runs a tabletop RPG session and needs to manage the game while keeping it moving for the players.

## Fabula Ultima knowledge

For this persona, assume the GM knows Fabula Ultima, including:

- [Checks](../../spec/fabula-ultima/checks.md): attribute dice, check results, fumbles and critical successes.
- [Weapon attacks](../../spec/fabula-ultima/weapon-attacks.md): accuracy, Defense, high roll, damage and damage types.
- [Defense and equipment](../../spec/fabula-ultima/defense-and-equipment.md): how attributes, armor and shields affect defenses and initiative, including the distinction between base and current dice.
- [NPCs and enemies](../../spec/fabula-ultima/npcs-and-enemies.md): NPC creation and types, combat statistics, abilities and enemy ranks, along with the rulebook's encounter guidance.

This is an assumption for persona exercises, not a claim that every GM knows these rules or that a real participant has demonstrated this knowledge. Familiarity with digital tools and with rules outside the linked specifications is unspecified.
