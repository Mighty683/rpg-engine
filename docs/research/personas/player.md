# Player — provisional persona

**Status:** Simulated, not based on participant interviews. Created 2026-11-06.

## Role and goals

The player participates in a tabletop RPG game and manages their character while being able to view the other characters. The intended journey lets them join a game, create or receive a character, edit assigned sheets, and view other sheets ([user stories](../../tasks/gm-player-separation/story.md), [UX journey](../../tasks/gm-player-separation/ux.md)).

## Working assumptions to validate

- **Context:** The player may need to use the application while following a conversation and making decisions during a session.
- **Behavior:** They may switch between their own sheet and other characters to understand the current situation, and return to their own sheet to make changes.
- **Needs:** A clear way to join the right game, find their character, understand which sheets they can edit, and see whether changes have saved.
- **Likely friction:** Uncertainty about how to obtain a character, which character is active, why a sheet is read-only, or whether a change is visible to the group.
- **Experience:** Familiarity with tabletop RPGs, the selected system, and digital character sheets is unknown. Neither expertise nor inexperience should be assumed.

These are hypotheses for design and evaluation, not observed behavior or validated requirements. The stated journey describes the intended product, not how real players necessarily work.

## Evidence and limitations

- [GM and player separation user stories](../../tasks/gm-player-separation/story.md), created 2026-11-06: states the intended player goal and scope; it is not participant research.
- [GM and player separation UX journey](../../tasks/gm-player-separation/ux.md), created 2026-11-06: describes proposed joining, character control, and recovery flows; it says the flow has not been tested with players.
- [UX guidelines](../../spec/ux.md), accessed 2026-11-06: describes broad role boundaries and product direction. Its statement that player workflows are deferred predates or conflicts with the more specific player journey above.

No player interviews or direct participant observations were found. This profile does not represent all players, and simulated use cannot validate it.

## Questions for research

1. How do players currently join games and obtain or prepare character sheets, and where do they need help from the GM?
2. What character information and actions do they need during play, and how do these vary by system or session format?
3. How do players expect assigned, active, and read-only characters to work?
4. What feedback do they need when a change saves, fails, or is made by someone else?
5. Which devices and existing tools do they use, and what interruptions or recovery options matter to them?
