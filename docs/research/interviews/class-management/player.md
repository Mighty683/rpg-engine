# Class management — simulated player review

**Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Persona:** [Provisional player](../../personas/player.md)  
**Status:** Simulated desk review; no player was interviewed. [Method and limitations](README.md).

## First walkthrough and predicted feedback

- **Find and interpret benefits:** Rowan's sample identifies Guardian as the source, labels one modifier Applied and another benefit Text, and shows Maximum HP changing from 45 to 50. A player may not know whether “Text” means a passive rule to interpret or an effect already applied by the engine.
- **Understand a skill:** The sample shows only free benefits. It does not show a skill benefit, rank, choices, or how a player reaches the list from their character sheet. The prototype cannot establish whether the player can find or understand those details during play.
- **Changes during a game:** The GM's removal screen lists affected characters, but the player view does not show how a player learns that a skill or benefit changed. Notification and permissions are not specified by this task; they need investigation rather than an assumed solution.

## Iteration and second review

The character screen now explains that applied modifiers change stats and text benefits describe effects to interpret during play. Source inspection confirms the explanation is next to the benefits. The sample remains illustrative: its numbers are not a verified rules calculation, and the static screen cannot test navigation, accessibility, updates, or comprehension. A skill-sourced example and rank progression require design against the rules before they can be evaluated.

## Questions for real players

1. Can they locate benefits on their sheet and explain which ones affect a stat automatically and which require interpretation?
2. Can they trace a benefit to a class or acquired skill and understand any rank or choice involved?
3. What information would help when a GM changes or removes a skill, and when would they expect to see it?
4. Is the information usable during play on their devices, including when viewing a read-only sheet?
