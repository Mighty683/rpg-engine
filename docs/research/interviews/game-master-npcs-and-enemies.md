# Simulated interview: game master — NPCs and enemies

**Date:** 2026-11-09

**Method:** A single AI author generated both questions and hypothetical answers using the [provisional GM persona](../personas/game-master.md) and [Fabula Ultima NPC rules](../../spec/fabula-ultima/npcs-and-enemies.md). The scenario assumes a GM familiar with those rules for this exercise; that expertise is not established for the persona.

**Status:** Simulated, not a participant interview or application test. The responses are invented predictions, not quotations from a real person or evidence of demand.

## Scenario and simulated responses

1. **What would you prepare for an upcoming encounter?** A GM might want to describe who the NPC is, then keep its combat profile, attacks, abilities and resources together. Some NPCs may matter to the story without needing combat statistics yet.
2. **What happens when the party fights someone unexpected?** A GM might adapt an existing profile or enter only what is needed initially, returning to details later. The preferred amount of structure and tolerance for incomplete profiles need research.
3. **What makes an enemy different from another NPC?** A combatant can be an ally or an opponent depending on the scene. Rank and type affect its rules; treating an enemy as permanently hostile could obscure that distinction. Whether GMs want a separate enemy category is unknown.
4. **What would help during play?** A GM might need to find a profile and change current HP or MP while keeping its maximum and abilities visible. The priority of live tracking relative to preparation, and whether separate copies are needed for repeated enemies, is untested.
5. **What about secrets and corrections?** A GM might want clarity about what players can see and a way to correct a profile without losing context. The desired visibility, recovery and sharing model is unresolved.

## Interpretation — hypotheses

A useful story should cover preparing and using NPCs and opponents while leaving the representation, automation, privacy model and encounter workflow open for later research and design. Rulebook guidance does not show how actual GMs work. This simulation supplies possible questions, not validated requirements or an independent interview.

## Research questions

- How do GMs create, reuse and find NPCs before and during play, including noncombat NPCs?
- How do they manage multiple instances, resource changes and changing allegiances?
- Which details do they share with players, and when?
- Which rules do they want calculated, manually entered or overridden, and what happens when a profile is incomplete?
- What would they adopt first, and what tools would they retain?
