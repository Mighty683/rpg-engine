# What is missing at the table?

**2026-11-27 · Simulated persona walkthroughs, not participant interviews.**

One reviewer used two isolated desktop Chromium sessions (1440 × 900) on the running app at `http://10.0.0.1:5173`. Both accounts opened the seeded **The Lantern Road** campaign. Each role was assessed against its own task before comparing views. The reviewer inspected existing data and opened roll dialogs without submitting rolls or changing campaign records.

| Role and report | Task | First question to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Game master](game-master.md) · `demo-gm` | Brief the party and adjudicate a seeded attack | After an accuracy roll, where does the GM keep the target and outcome? |
| [Player](player.md) · `demo-player` | Rejoin the scene and act as Nera Vale | Can a returning player recover the party’s next objective without interrupting play? |

Priority reflects where an interruption could affect the whole table, not measured frequency or demand. This was one campaign on desktop, without a live group. Joining, mobile use, save failures and concurrent edits were not tested. The [earlier GM](../game-master-application-review.md) and [player](../player-application-review.md) reviews describe an earlier version.
