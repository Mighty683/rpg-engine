# Fabula Ultima — checks

## Dice and modifiers

A check rolls exactly two independent dice based on the character’s current attributes. The standard attribute die sizes are d6, d8, d10 and d12. If the same attribute appears twice, roll its current die size twice. Temporary effects that change an attribute’s die size apply before rolling. Keep base attributes and temporary changes separate.

Add applicable signed modifiers to the sum of the two faces. Modifiers do not change either face or the high roll. The high roll is the higher individual face. Double 1 is a fumble. Matching faces of 6 or higher are a critical success. Determine both from the faces before modifiers. A regular check against a Difficulty Level succeeds when its result meets or exceeds the difficulty, subject to criticals, fumbles and specific effects.

## Opposed checks

Each participant rolls the same attribute pair using their own current dice and applicable modifiers. There is no fixed Difficulty Level. A critical success ranks above an ordinary result, and a fumble ranks below one. Compare modified totals between ordinary results. Two criticals or two fumbles tie regardless of their totals. Participants tied for the highest result repeat the check until there is a winner.

A weapon attack normally compares its Accuracy Check against the target’s Defense. Resolve it under the [weapon attack rules](weapon-attacks.md). A single die or a generic dice pool can be rolled as a table utility. Fabula check outcomes require a valid two-die check.

Rules references: [Press Start character kit](https://d1vzi28wh99zvq.cloudfront.net/pdf_previews/411240-sample.pdf) and _Fabula Ultima Core Rulebook_, “Performing an Attribute Check” and “Opposed Checks”, pp. 42 and 48.
