# Fabula Ultima — NPCs and enemies

This is a focused analysis of the supplied Polish core rulebook, not a complete rules implementation or a catalog of creatures and abilities. Page numbers below are printed book pages; PDF pages are two higher. NPCs can be allies or enemies, and not every narrative NPC needs a combat profile.

## Creating a combat NPC

The GM can adapt an existing bestiary profile or build a soldier from scratch. Choose the NPC's role in the world and four traits, level 5–60, and one of eight types. Assign DEX, INS, MIG and WLP dice using one of these arrays: d8/d8/d8/d8, d10/d8/d8/d6, d10/d10/d6/d6, or d12/d8/d6/d6. At levels 20, 40 and 60, increase one die by one step, up to d12. Traits support roleplaying; villains can invoke them to reroll checks. (pp. 302–303.)

A basic attack has a name, melee or ranged reach, two chosen attributes for accuracy, high roll + 5 damage and a damage type. NPCs can have multiple attacks, abilities, spells and equipment, subject to their rules. Each type provides starting abilities; add one per ten levels. Additional vulnerabilities can buy abilities: one per vulnerability, or two for vulnerability to physical damage. Type rules and limited abilities still apply. (pp. 302–307.)

For an unmodified soldier, calculate initiative as (base DEX die size + base INS die size) / 2, applying armor penalties; maximum HP as twice level + five times base MIG die size; crisis as half maximum HP; maximum MP as level + five times base WLP die size; Defense from current DEX and Magic Defense from current INS. Equipment and abilities may change these values. NPCs add floor(level / 10) to accuracy and magic checks; their attacks and spells gain +5 damage at level 20, +10 at 40, and +15 at 60. Keep base dice distinct from current dice. (p. 303; see also [defense and equipment](defense-and-equipment.md) and [weapon attacks](weapon-attacks.md).)

## Enemy ranks and encounter guidance

A soldier is the baseline. An elite counts as two soldiers of the same level: double maximum HP, add one chosen ability, grant two turns per round and add 2 initiative. A champion replaces a chosen number of soldiers: multiply maximum HP by that number, double maximum MP, add that many chosen abilities, grant that many turns per round and add that number to initiative. Extra turns still alternate with player characters while a hero has a turn remaining. (pp. 294–295.)

For encounter guidance, party level is the highest level of a participating player character. The book suggests enemy levels up to five above party level for an easy fight and up to ten above for a challenging one; lower or 11+ higher may be too weak or too strong. As a separate guide, use one fewer soldier than player characters for an easy fight, the same number for normal, and one more for difficult; an allied NPC with its own turn counts as another hero. These are guidance, not guarantees: party abilities, equipment and current resources matter. (pp. 293–294, 302.)

## Scope and follow-up

The rulebook also describes NPC abilities and spells (pp. 306 onward), villains, affinities, status effects, conflict turns, rewards and bestiary profiles. This analysis does not transcribe their full rules or establish automation, visibility, persistence, encounter design, or a complete NPC lifecycle.
