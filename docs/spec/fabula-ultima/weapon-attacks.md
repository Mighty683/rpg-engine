# Fabula Ultima — weapon attacks

## Accuracy and damage

Accuracy uses the standard [two-die check rules](checks.md). The target supplies a Defense score for the attack.

A weapon specifies the two attributes used for its accuracy check, an accuracy modifier, a damage formula and damage type. Attribute values are dice sizes; roll one current attribute die for each attribute specified by the weapon. Keep the individual results as well as their sum.

Add the applicable accuracy modifiers to the sum. A normal weapon attack hits when the result equals or exceeds the target's Defense. A pair of ones is a fumble and automatically fails; matching dice showing six or higher are a critical success and automatically hit. Specific rules or effects may change the check or its outcome.

For a successful ordinary weapon attack, the high roll is the higher number shown on the two dice. Apply the weapon's damage formula, usually high roll plus a fixed damage bonus. Accuracy modifiers are not added to the high roll. Apply relevant skills, other effects and the target's affinity according to their rules; do not assume missing effects are zero or invent stacking rules.

Example: the supplied rapier uses DEX + INS + 1 for accuracy and high roll + 6 physical damage. Rolling 6 and 5 gives an accuracy result of 12; against Defense 12 it hits, for 12 damage before other effects. Keep the two dice independent; neither their sum nor the accuracy bonus is the high roll.

## Boundaries

A weapon's rules data must retain both attribute choices and its accuracy bonus separately from damage and character attribute values. Resolve attacks against the applicable game and rules configuration. Do not present an incomplete calculation as final when required rules data is unavailable. This specification does not define spell attacks, equipment eligibility, special abilities or a complete combat engine.
